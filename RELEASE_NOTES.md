# NewYearTree v1.0.0

Bring a Christmas tree to your Windows desktop, complete with sparkling lights, gently swaying ornaments, falling snow and a live countdown to New Year.

## Included

- English and Russian interface.
- Countdown in days, hours and minutes, based on the Windows local clock.
- Five light modes and three color palettes.
- Adjustable light brightness and speed.
- Twelve ornaments with optional gentle swaying.
- Light, Medium and Heavy snowfall.
- Snow-covered branches and presents on a snowy base.
- Resizing, dragging, position lock and always-on-top mode.
- Automatically saved settings.
- One portable EXE with all graphics embedded.

## Get started

Download **NewYearTree.exe** from the assets below and double-click it. No installation is required. You do not need the source-code ZIP to run the widget.

- Drag with the left mouse button to move the tree.
- Double-click to open settings.
- Right-click to open the menu.
- To switch to English, open settings and select **English** under **Язык / Language**.
- Choose **Exit** from the menu to close the widget.

Requires Windows with .NET Framework 4.x. The app works offline.

The countdown format is **days:hours:minutes**. Settings and position are saved in your Windows user profile.
