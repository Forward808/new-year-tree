# NewYearTree — User Guide

NewYearTree is a Christmas tree widget for your Windows desktop. It includes sparkling lights, gently moving ornaments, falling snow, presents and a countdown to New Year.

## Start

Download `NewYearTree.exe` from the project's GitHub release and double-click it. No installation is needed. All graphics are included in the EXE, and the app works offline. Windows with .NET Framework 4.x is required.

## Choose English

Double-click the tree to open settings. At the top, find **Язык / Language** and choose **English**. The interface changes immediately. Russian is also available.

## Move and customize

Drag the tree with the left mouse button to move it. Double-click to open settings, or right-click to open the menu.

In settings, you can change the size, light colors, brightness and animation speed. Choose one of five light modes, turn ornament swaying on or off, and select Light, Medium or Heavy snowfall. You can also hide the countdown or ornaments, keep the tree above other windows, or lock its position.

The countdown shows **days:hours:minutes** until January 1 at midnight. It uses your Windows local clock and automatically switches to the following New Year when the year changes.

## Hide, recover or exit

Right-click the tree or its notification area icon to open the menu. Choose **Show / hide tree** to toggle visibility, **Bring tree back on screen** to recover it, or **Exit** to close it.

If the notification icon is not visible, open the overflow menu next to the Windows clock.

## Preferences

Changes are saved automatically. The settings file is located at:

```text
%LOCALAPPDATA%\NewYearTree\settings.xml
```

To reset all preferences, close the widget and delete that file. To update the app, exit the old version before replacing its EXE. Your saved preferences will remain available.
